Privacy Policy for Chatooz

Effective Date: September 25, 2026

Welcome to Chatooz. Your privacy and trust are our utmost priority. This Privacy Policy describes how Chatooz ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, protects, and discloses your personal data when you use the Chatooz mobile application and related communication services.

1. Information We Collect

To provide secure real-time messaging, audio/video calling, and social status features, Chatooz collects the following information:

  • Account Information: When you register or log in, we collect your phone number or email address solely to send one-time verification passcodes (OTP) for account security and authentication.
  • Contacts List (Optional): With your explicit consent, Chatooz accesses your device contacts to help you identify which of your contacts are already using Chatooz. We do not sell, rent, or share your contact list with any advertising or third-party marketing entities.
  • Camera & Microphone: We request access to your device camera and microphone strictly when you initiate or receive voice and video calls, record voice messages, or capture photos/videos to send inside chats or update your profile picture.
  • Photos, Media & Storage: Storage access is requested exclusively to allow you to send documents, photos, audio, and videos to other users within your active chats.
  • Device & Network Information: We collect non-identifiable technical data such as operating system version, connection type, and network latency to maintain stable WebRTC real-time audio/video calls.

2. Use of Foreground Services and Full-Screen Intents

Chatooz uses foreground services and full-screen intent permissions strictly to:

  • Maintain real-time voice and video call connections without call drops when the app is minimized or the screen is locked.
  • Display incoming full-screen call ringing notifications so you do not miss calls from your contacts.
  • Synchronize pending chat messages reliably in real-time.

3. Data Protection and Security

We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (including WebRTC DTLS/SRTP encryption for real-time media streams and TLS/HTTPS for server communication) to protect your conversations from unauthorized interception or access.

4. Third-Party Sharing

Chatooz does not sell, trade, or monetize your personal information. We do not serve third-party targeted behavioral advertisements, nor do we share your data with data brokers.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You have full control over your data:

  • You can delete individual messages, clear chat histories, or delete your statuses at any time directly within the application.
  • You can request full deletion of your account and associated profile data by contacting our support team at v8929731578@gmail.com.

6. Children's Privacy

Chatooz is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
